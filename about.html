About Me .

Where I Come From .

My name is Alexander Gonzalez, and I was born and raised in Katy, Texas, just west of Houston. As a kid, I grew up building LEGO sets and drawing my biggest ideas on my homework. It didn't improve my grades, but I couldn't help letting my creativity flow.

I was in a STEM program in high school, where I learned about design and team-based work environments. But I didn't get my first real taste of engineering until I got to college.

My first stop was the University of Texas at San Antonio (Go Runners!). There, I developed a love for the motorsports industry while working closely with the Formula SAE (FSAE) team. From there, I decided to pursue motorsports further by transferring to the University of North Carolina at Charlotte. That's where things really started. At UNC Charlotte, I developed suspension systems for the 2024 electric race car, solidified my love for motorsports, and became curious about aerodynamics.

Lastly, I decided to come back home to Texas and finish my degree at the University of Texas at Tyler. I'm currently completing my bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering and truly immersing myself in the practice of the profession.

All in all, I appreciate you taking the time to hear my story. I hope you enjoy the research, development, and ideas shared throughout this website. Thank you.
